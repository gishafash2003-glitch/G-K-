facts about dogs

  • Dogs have an incredible sense of smell, which is why they can be trained to detect diseases like cancer and can smell human emotions. They have a unique way of cooling down by sweating through their paws and panting, and while they can see some colors, they don't see them the same way humans do
  • Dogs are known for their loyalty and companionship,
  • They are highly social animals and thrive on interaction with humans and other dogs.
  • Dogs come in a wide variety of breeds, each with its own unique characteristics and traits.
